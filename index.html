Privacy Policy

Morse Code (also known as MorseBlink)
Effective date: November 7, 2026

This policy explains how the Morse Code Android app handles information when you encode text into Morse code light signals or read light signals with your device camera.

Information the app handles

  • Camera frames: If you start the Morse reader, the app analyzes camera brightness on your device to detect light pulses. Frames are processed for this purpose only; the app does not save or transmit them.
  • Text and decoded messages: Text you enter for encoding and text decoded from light pulses are processed on your device. The app does not send this content to the developer or other parties.
  • Settings: Your selected flash color, output method, and app language are stored locally on your device. They are removed when you clear app data or uninstall the app.
  • Camera permission: Android requires camera permission for the Morse reader and, on supported devices, for controlling the device flashlight. Camera access is used only after you choose a relevant feature. You can manage permission in Android settings.

Collection, sharing, and security

The current app does not include an account system, advertising, analytics, or network requests. It does not collect or share personal data with the developer or third parties. App data stays on your device, where Android's device security controls apply.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It has no account or communication features.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when a revision is made.
